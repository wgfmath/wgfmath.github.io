Distance Seidel Energy under Edge Deletion in Complete Multipartite Graphs

Guangfu Wang, Xiaoling Zhang

Abstract

Let G be a complete multipartite graph and let e be an edge such that G - e is connected. This manuscript proves that deleting e strictly increases the distance Seidel energy, settling a problem of Haritha and Chithra for complete bipartite graphs and extending it to every complete multipartite graph, including parts of size one. It also shows that the single-edge hypothesis is essential by giving a matching-deletion counterexample.
