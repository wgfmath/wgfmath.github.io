A Vanishing Proportion of Metrizable Path Systems on 2-Connected Graphs

Abstract

For a finite connected graph G, let P(G) be the set of consistent path systems and M(G) the systems induced by positive edge weights. This manuscript answers a question of Cizma and Linial by constructing a sequence of 2-connected graphs for which |M(G)| / |P(G)| tends to zero. The proof combines explicit families of consistent systems, a compact encoding by vertex types and pair colorings, and a probabilistic obstruction argument.

Keywords
