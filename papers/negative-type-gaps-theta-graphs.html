Abstract

This manuscript studies quantitative obstructions to negative type in graph metrics. It proves the sharp normalized negative type gap 1/24 for theta-containing metric graphs when every reduced edge segment has length at least one, and shows that the vertex shortest-path metric of the 3-subdivision of any finite graph with a K2,3-minor violates negative type.
